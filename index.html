Our Story

Built on the meaning
of home.

RumaNurse was born from a simple belief: that every person deserves to feel at home in their own care. As a California LVN, I've seen what happens when families have to choose between clinical competence and genuine warmth. I built RumaNurse so they never have to.

The heart of our name · The soul of our care

This is a family practice, built with intention. Every client becomes part of our circle — not a case number, not a time slot. We take on a small number of families because exceptional care cannot be scaled.
